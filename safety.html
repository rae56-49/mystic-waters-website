SAFETY GUIDELINES & FAQs

🛟 Safety Guidelines

  • ✔ Wear Approved Safety Gear

    All participants must wear life jackets or appropriate safety equipment during water activities.

  • ✔ Follow Instructor Directions

    Always listen carefully to your guide or instructor and follow their instructions.

  • ✔ Know Your Limits

    Choose activities suited to your fitness and swimming ability. Inform us of any medical conditions before participating.

  • ✔ No Alcohol or Drugs

    Participation is not allowed under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

  • ✔ Children Supervision

    Children under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian at all times.

Frequently Asked Questions

📌 Do I need to know how to swim to participate?

Not necessarily. Many of our activities are beginner-friendly, and all safety gear is provided.

📌 Are activities suitable for kids?

Yes! We offer a range of family-friendly water sports with safety measures in place.

📌 Are your guides certified?

Yes, all guides are trained in water safety, first aid, and CPR.

📌 What’s the cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 24 hours before the activity are eligible for a full refund.

༄.° Our History.

Founded in the heart of Jamaica’s beautiful coast, Mystic Water Watersports began with a passion for adventure and a deep love for the ocean. What started as a small local operation offering paddleboard rentals has grown into a thriving watersports destination — welcoming thrill-seekers, families, and explorers from around the world. Over the years, Mystic Water has earned a reputation for providing safe, unforgettable experiences on the water, blending excitement with the natural beauty of the Caribbean.





༄.° Our Mission.

At Mystic Water Watersports, our mission is to inspire adventure and create lasting memories through safe, fun, and eco-friendly watersport experiences. Whether you’re gliding across calm waters or conquering ocean waves, we aim to offer every guest a deep connection to nature, a sense of accomplishment, and pure joy.






༄.° Our Values.

• Safety First We place the highest priority on the safety and well-being of our guests and crew. Our equipment is maintained to the highest standards, and all our instructors are professionally trained and certified.

• Sustainability Mystic Water is committed to protecting the natural marine environment. We follow eco-friendly practices and support local conservation efforts to ensure the ocean remains vibrant for future generations.

• Passion for Adventure We believe that life is best lived in motion. Whether it’s kayaking, jet-skiing, or snorkeling, we design every activity to spark excitement and curiosity.

• Exceptional Service We take pride in delivering friendly, professional service — treating each guest like part of the Mystic Water family.
